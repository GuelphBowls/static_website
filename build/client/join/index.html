Joining is Easy

New and returning members can bring their payment and completed membership form to the club house during the season. Don’t forget to read and fill out the insurance waiver. We will gladly receive your forms and payment via email or in person at the club.


guelphlbcsecretary@gmail.com

If you’re a returning member who has already completed an insurance waiver, you only need to submit the membership form.

New Members

Feel free to drop by for Open Bowl Mondays at 7pm (just ask for Blake) or book an appointment by emailing guelphlbcevents@gmail.com.


So, what are you waiting for? If you haven’t already joined, give it a try. You’re welcome to come out a time or two to try lawn bowling. Your best bet is to visit us on an open bowling night. We’ve got lots of enthusiastic members who will be happy to introduce you to the game.

No Equipment Needed

All you have to do is come out and play. We have a supply of lawn bowls for members and guests to use. It’s that easy! Just don’t forget sunscreen, a hat, and something non-alcoholic to keep hydrated.


We do, however, ask bowlers to wear flat, soft-sole shoes to play. We prefer club members wear shoes that are worn for lawn bowling only as opposed to street shoes.
