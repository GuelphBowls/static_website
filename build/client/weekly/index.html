Monday

New Bowlers Friendly

7:00pm - 10:00pm

Start your week with a relaxed game! Join us every Monday evening for our Friendly Social Bowl — perfect for new bowlers. No experience needed, and if you're new, your first session is free. Come meet the community and try something new on the green!

Tuesday

4321 Social Bowl - $2

7:00pm - 10:00pm

Test your skills in a fast-paced game of 4321! Join us Tuesday nights for light competition and community fun. Just $2 to play—open to all skill levels. Come solo or bring a friend!


Matches arranged according to attendance.

Wednesday

Singles

7:00pm - 10:00pm

Come enjoy a friendly game of singles in a welcoming, social atmosphere. This night is all about fun, not competition. Great for bowlers who want to focus on their game at their own pace.


* The first Wednesday of each month the greens are closed for maintenance.

Thursday

Motley - $2

7:00pm - 10:00pm

Join us for a relaxed evening of short-format lawn bowling! Each game is just 5 ends, keeping things quick and fun with rotating partners and opponents.

Just $2 to play, and prizes every 4 weeks.


Format adjusts based on turnout—perfect for casual players looking for variety and a good time.

Friday

Open Bowling & Special Events

Time varies based on event

Drop by and bowl at your own pace! Our Open Bowling sessions are perfect for casual roll-ups or bringing friends to try the game.


Keep an eye out for Special Events—from theme nights to fun challenges—there's always something different happening on the green. All are welcome!

Saturday

Open Bowling & Tournaments

Time varies based on event

Whether you're in the mood for a relaxed roll-up or ready to test your skills, we've got you covered! Open Bowling lets you play at your own pace, while scheduled Tournaments offer a chance for friendly competition. Check the calendar for upcoming dates—everyone's welcome!

Sunday

Youth Bowls

3:30pm Start

Begins June 28th

A fantastic opportunity for young players to get into lawn bowling! Our Youth Bowling sessions offer hands-on instruction from experienced coaches in a fun and supportive environment. Perfect for beginners—come learn the game and build skills while having a great time on the green!
